Interests & Background

Born and raised in Pacific Palisades, California, KJ grew up competing in one of the most talent-rich basketball environments in the country — the Los Angeles prep circuit. That early exposure to elite competition shaped his relentless mentality and scoring instincts from a young age.

Beyond the game, KJ is deeply invested in his growth as a player and a person. He is motivated by the culture and community at BYU, describing the Provo environment as a place where "something special is being built." On his own playing style, KJ describes himself as "an athletic point guard who can score, get teammates involved, and be a defensive dog" — a mentality he brings every time he steps on the floor.

KJ is active on social media (@kjxperry) where he shares his basketball journey and connects with fans. He is passionate about competing at the highest level and is focused on developing his three-point game and strength as he prepares for his first full collegiate season.
